## 🚀 How to Start Fresh

### Step 1: Clear All Existing Products (Optional)

Open browser console (F12) and run:
```javascript

products = [];
render();
console.log('✅ All products cleared!');
```

### Step 2: Access Product Management

1. **Login as Admin**
   - Email: `israelezrakisakye@gmail.com`
   - Password: (your admin password)

2. **Open Product Management**
   - Click on **Profile** tab (bottom navigation)
   - Click **"📦 Manage Products"** button

### Step 3: Add Your First Product

1. Click **"+ Add Product"** button
2. Fill in the form:
   - **📸 Product Image**: Click "Choose from Gallery" and select image from phone
   - **Product Name**: e.g., "Classic Navy Suit"
   - **Category**: Select from dropdown (Men Suits, Office Wear, etc.)
   - **Price**: Enter price in UGX (e.g., 500000)
   - **Sizes**: Check all available sizes

3. Click **"Add Product"** button
4. Product is saved immediately!

## 🎯 Best Practices

### For Product Images:
- ✅ Use high-quality photos (max 5MB)
- ✅ Take photos with good lighting
- ✅ Show product clearly
- ✅ Use consistent background if possible

### For Product Names:
- ✅ Be descriptive: "Elegant Navy 3-Piece Suit"
- ✅ Include key features: "Slim Fit Office Blazer"
- ✅ Keep it professional

### For Pricing:
- ✅ Use whole numbers (no decimals)
- ✅ Price in UGX (e.g., 500000 for 500k)
- ✅ Be competitive

### For Sizes:
- ✅ Select all available sizes
- ✅ Use "One Size" or "Free Size" for accessories
- ✅ Check multiple sizes for clothing

## 🔧 Managing Products

### Edit a Product:
1. Go to Product Management
2. Find the product in the list
3. Click **"Edit"** button
4. Make changes
5. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Delete a Product:
1. Go to Product Management
2. Find the product
3. Click **"Del"** button
4. Confirm deletion

### Change Product Image:
1. Edit the product
2. Click **"Change Image"**
3. Select new image from gallery
4. Image uploads automatically
5. Click **"Save Changes"**

## 💾 Data Storage

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Image not showing after upload?
- Check browser console for errors
- Try refreshing the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try in incognito mode

### Product not saving?
- Check all required fields are filled
- Make sure at least one size is selected
- Check browser console for errors
