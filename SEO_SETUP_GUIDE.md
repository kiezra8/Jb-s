## How to Deploy

### Step 1: Upload Files
Upload these files to your website root directory:
1. `sitemap.xml`
2. `robots.txt`
3. Updated `index.html`

### Step 2: Verify Sitemap Access
After uploading, verify your sitemap is accessible:
- Visit: https://jbstailoredfashionz.com/sitemap.xml
- You should see the XML content

### Step 3: Submit to Google Search Console

1. **Go to Google Search Console**
   - Visit: https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Add your property: `https://jbstailoredfashionz.com`

2. **Verify Ownership**
   - Choose verification method (HTML file upload or meta tag)
   - Follow Google's instructions

3. **Submit Sitemap**
   - In Search Console, go to "Sitemaps"
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"

4. **Request Indexing**
   - Go to "URL Inspection"
   - Enter your homepage URL
   - Click "Request Indexing"

### Step 4: Submit to Bing Webmaster Tools

1. Visit: https://www.bing.com/webmasters
2. Add your site
3. Submit sitemap: `https://jbstailoredfashionz.com/sitemap.xml`
